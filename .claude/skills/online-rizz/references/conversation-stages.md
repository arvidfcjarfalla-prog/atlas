# Conversation Stages

The funnel from match to date. Diagnose which stage the thread is in, then play
the move for that stage. Don't skip stages, but don't linger either.

## 1. Opener
**Goal:** earn a reply and start a thread.
- Hook on something specific in their profile/photos.
- 1–3 sentences. End with an easy, interesting on-ramp.
- One opener, then let them respond — no double-texting yet.
- See `references/openers.md` for templates.

## 2. Rapport
**Goal:** find a real thread of connection.
- Build on their reply; trade small self-disclosures.
- Look for shared interests, recurring jokes, or a topic with energy.
- Keep it light. This is where you find the "callback" you'll reuse later.

## 3. Banter / tension
**Goal:** turn rapport into attraction.
- Playful teasing, light challenges, callbacks to earlier jokes.
- Show personality and a bit of edge — flirt, don't just chat.
- Signs it's working: longer replies, faster replies, they tease back,
  they ask about you.

## 4. Ask-out
**Goal:** move off the app to a real plan.
- Trigger: there's genuine back-and-forth + a hook to hang a plan on.
- Be specific and low-pressure: a concrete activity + a loose time, easy to
  accept or counter-propose.
- Tie it to the thread ("you clearly have opinions about coffee — let's settle
  it, free Thursday?").
- If they hesitate on logistics but seem keen, offer an alternative once. If
  they decline outright, back off warmly.

## 5. Re-engage (gone quiet)
**Goal:** revive a stalled thread — once, with low stakes.
- One light, specific re-engage tied to something from earlier or new (a
  callback, a relevant thing you saw). Not "hey" or "you ghosting me?".
- If no response or a flat one, stop. One revive attempt, not three.

## Diagnostic cheatsheet
| You see… | Stage | Move |
|---|---|---|
| Just matched, profile only | Opener | Specific hook + easy question |
| One or two exchanges, polite | Rapport | Build the thread, self-disclose |
| Jokes landing, fast replies | Banter | Tease, callback, flirt |
| Strong back-and-forth, a shared hook | Ask-out | Specific low-pressure plan |
| Replies slowed/stopped | Re-engage | One light callback, then let go |
