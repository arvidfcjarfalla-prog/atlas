# Do / Don't

Quick reference. Run every draft past this before sending.

## Do
- **Do** reference something specific to them.
- **Do** keep openers short (1–3 sentences).
- **Do** ask questions that are easy *and* fun to answer.
- **Do** trade self-disclosure for their answers (no interrogation).
- **Do** match their length, tone, and emoji level.
- **Do** tease playfully once rapport exists.
- **Do** move to a specific, low-pressure date once there's real back-and-forth.
- **Do** sound like a real person — contractions, casual, your actual voice.
- **Do** back off gracefully at any clear sign of disinterest.

## Don't
- **Don't** open with "hey" / "hi" / "how's your day" / generic compliments.
- **Don't** comment only on looks, especially sexually, early on.
- **Don't** send a wall of text or ten questions in a row.
- **Don't** double- or triple-text when they haven't replied.
- **Don't** use pet names ("babe", "hun") with a stranger.
- **Don't** neg, pressure, guilt-trip, or use false scarcity. Ever.
- **Don't** persist after a no or after clear disinterest.
- **Don't** lie, exaggerate, or invent facts about yourself.
- **Don't** chat forever and let the match go stale.
- **Don't** emoji-spam or use try-hard "pickup lines" that scream copy-paste.

## Tone calibration
| Register | When | Example feel |
|---|---|---|
| Warm/safe | First contact, unsure of vibe | curious, friendly, specific |
| Playful/teasing | Rapport established, they joke back | light edge, callbacks |
| Bold | Strong banter, mutual momentum | confident ask, flirtier |
