# Openers

Patterns, not scripts. Every opener must be rebuilt around a **specific hook**
from the actual profile. Templates are scaffolding — fill the brackets with real
detail, then make it sound like the user.

## The rule
> No "hey" / "hi" / "how's your day" / "you're gorgeous". Those are the most
> common openers and among the worst-performing. Reference something specific,
> be short, end with an on-ramp.

## By hook type

### Hook: a profile detail (trip, job, hobby)
- "Okay, [Lisbon] — best thing you ate there, go. I'm collecting a list."
- "A [pottery] person. Are you genuinely good or proudly terrible? Both are
  acceptable answers."
- "[Trail running] *and* you survived — what's the most lost you've ever gotten?"

### Hook: a photo
- "That [dog] is clearly the main character. What's the name and the attitude?"
- "Need the backstory on [the photo with the giant cake]. This feels important."

### Hook: a prompt answer (Hinge)
- Reply *to the prompt*, don't restate it: "Your hill to die on is [pineapple on
  pizza]? Bold. I respect the confidence, I question the judgment."

### Hook: shared interest
- "Finally someone else who [reads sci-fi]. Settle a debate — [Dune or
  Foundation]?"

### Hook: playful tease (use lightly, only on something safe)
- "I'm gonna need you to explain the [third photo]. No notes, just questions."

## Structure that works
1. **Observation** — name the specific thing.
2. **Reaction** — a small opinion, joke, or curiosity (shows personality).
3. **On-ramp** — an easy, fun question or a light challenge.

Example assembled: *"[Observation] You've been to Japan three times. [Reaction]
Either a deep obsession or excellent taste. [On-ramp] What keeps pulling you
back?"*

## Calibration notes
- Length: 1–3 sentences. Shorter on Tinder, slightly more on Hinge prompts.
- Tone: warm + curious by default; teasing only when there's a safe target.
- Emoji: at most one, and only if it adds tone. Never emoji-spam.
- Never lead with sexual content or looks-only comments.
- If their profile is sparse, hook on the single best photo or ask a fun,
  specific would-you-rather tied to something visible.
