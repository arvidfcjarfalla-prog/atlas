03 How it works

Three steps, in this order, every time.

  1. Intake

    You describe what you make in a short form. Hearth drafts the first claim sentence and the mechanical paragraph below it. You read what it wrote.

  2. Edit

    You click on any sentence on the live page and rewrite it in place. There is no separate editor view. What you see is what is published.

  3. Publish

    The page lives at yourname.hearth.page from the moment you save. A custom domain is a one-time setting you can add later.

The structure is fixed. One claim, one proof section, one contact point. There is no template marketplace and no drag-and-drop editor. If you need a homepage, an about page, and a blog with categories, you need a different tool.
